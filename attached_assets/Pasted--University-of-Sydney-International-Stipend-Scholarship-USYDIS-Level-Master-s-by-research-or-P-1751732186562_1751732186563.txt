✅ What To Do Next
Check eligibility – Confirm your degree type (research/coursework), academic standing, nationality, and funding needs.

Prepare for automatic consideration – VCIS and Business School scholarships require an unconditional offer by key dates; no separate application needed.

Contact global partners – For equity scholarships, you must be nominated by one of the University’s partner organizations – check that eligibility.

Prepare paperwork – Have proof of academic excellence, research potential (for USYDIS), and your OSHC plan ready.

Review deadlines – Especially for USYDIS (Sept/Dec) and VCIS rounds throughout 2025–2026.
